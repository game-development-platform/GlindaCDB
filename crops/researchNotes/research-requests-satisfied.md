# Research requests: satisfied

Requests from [research-requests.md](research-requests.md) that have been
closed: the work was read and used (**Read**), or it couldn't be had
(**Not obtained**). Each entry keeps its request details and says where the
work was used. The documents themselves are never in this repository.

## Closed requests

- [x] **`mandarin--uc-cost-sjv-2011`**: O'Connell, Kallsen, Klonsky,
  De Moura and Tumber, *Sample Costs to Establish a Citrus Orchard and
  Produce Mandarins (Tango), San Joaquin Valley – South, 2011* (UC
  Cooperative Extension / UC Davis Agricultural and Resource Economics).
  - **Where:** coststudies.ucdavis.edu → Archived studies → commodity
    "Mandarin" (the site returns 403 to scripts). A newer mandarin study
    for any California region is also useful if it exists.
  - **Needed for:** trees per acre, yields by orchard age, year of first
    harvest, labour hours by operation, harvest cost, applied water,
    productive life.
  - **Result:** Read 2026-11-20; used in mandarin.md [12].
- [x] **`mandarin--uc-cost-ventura-2005`**: UC Cooperative Extension,
  *Sample Costs to Establish and Produce Mandarins, Ventura County*
  (2004-05).
  - **Where:** same site, archived "Mandarin" studies.
  - **Needed for:** the 14 × 18 ft / 170 trees per acre spacing (now
    snippet-only), yields by age, labour hours.
  - **Result:** Read 2026-11-20; used in mandarin.md [13].
- [x] **`mandarin--ufifas-cg095`**: UF/IFAS EDIS CG095, *Florida Citrus
  Production Guide: Cold Protection* (or its current title).
  - **Where:** https://edis.ifas.ufl.edu/publication/CG095. The
    edis/ask.ifas.ufl.edu redirect loops for scripts; the PDF link is
    https://edis.ifas.ufl.edu/publication/CG095/pdf.
  - **Needed for:** fruit and tree freeze temperatures and durations by
    citrus type, including mandarin and tangerine (now snippet-only).
  - **Result:** Read 2026-11-20 (2026-2029 edition, now CMG18); used in mandarin.md [14]. It gives no freeze-threshold temperatures.
- [x] **`mandarin--ufifas-dancy`**: Dancy Tangerine fact sheet (from the
  topic page). Needed for tree size, bearing age, yield and cold tolerance
  of the standard US tangerine.
  - **Result:** Read 2026-11-20 (HS169, revised February 2024); used in
    mandarin.md [15]. No yield, bearing-age or temperature figures.
- [x] **`mandarin--ufifas-sunburst`**: Sunburst fact sheet. Needed for
  self-infertility and pollinizer choice.
  - **Result:** Read 2026-11-20 (HS168, revised February 2024); used in
