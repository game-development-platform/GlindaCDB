# Checklist additions log

Tracks when `skills/crop-research/SKILL.md`'s Step 3 research checklist and
the `templates/research-doc.md` "Agronomic data" bullets gain a new,
broadly-applicable research angle. Each entry here is the reason a batch of
status-table rows was moved from `RESEARCHED` to `OUT-OF-DATE` on that date -
not every crop will have something to add for every angle below, but every
`OUT-OF-DATE` row from a listed batch should be checked against the angles in
that entry when it's re-researched.

## 2026-11-08 — first checklist expansion

Reviewed against a private source project's own accumulated list of
recurring crop-trait patterns (never itself referenced from this project -
see `skills/crop-research/SKILL.md`'s "don't reference anything outside this
project" rule). Nine angles were added that the original checklist didn't
explicitly prompt for:

1. **Chill-hours/vernalization requirement.**
2. **Photoperiod sensitivity** - both for flowering and, separately, for
   tuberization/bulbing/rhizome yield itself (a short-day/long-day
   requirement for yield is a different claim from one for flowering).
3. **Frost as a quality event, not only a hazard** - can improve a crop
   (e.g. sweetening) as well as damage it, and both can be true for the same
   crop at different times.
4. **Flooded/paddy water requirement**, as distinct from ordinary
   irrigation need.
5. **Clonal/viral degeneration risk** in vegetatively-propagated crops, and
   how growers get clean stock.
6. **Salinity tolerance** as its own soil dimension, separate from pH.
7. **Elevation** as a climate dimension that ordinary hardiness-zone/
   climate-zone language doesn't capture.
8. **Ergot/fungal contamination** as a hazard distinct from plant-produced
   toxins.
9. **Asynchronous/successive-ripening harvest** as a pattern distinct from
   ordinary single-harvest or cut-and-come-again (not every unit on the
   plant ready at once, within one planting).

**Rows affected:** every row that was `RESEARCHED` as of 2026-11-08 across
every category status table was moved to `OUT-OF-DATE` on this date, since
all of them predate this expansion - 215 rows total, across `fruit` (104),
`spice-herb` (43), `root-tuber` (43), `oil` (17), `nut` (5), `vegetable` (2),
`medicinal` (1). This includes `orange`, `bitter_orange`, and `chinotto`
specifically, even though the latter two were written earlier the same day,
since they also predate this change and weren't written against it.

No category had any `RESEARCHED` rows before this date in: `algae`,
`cereal`, `fiber`, `forage`, `fungi`, `legume`, `stimulant`, `sugar`.

## 2026-11-11 — invasiveness/biosecurity

Prompted by algae research (wakame is a listed invasive in several regions,
and sugar kelp/giant kelp cultivation is restricted to local genetic stock in
some jurisdictions), but the angle applies across every category. One angle
added, to both Step 3's checklist and a new "Invasiveness / biosecurity" line
in the template's "Agronomic data" section:

1. **Invasiveness/biosecurity** - native vs. introduced range; any
   invasive-species listing or formal assessment (naming the list and
   jurisdiction); weedy/volunteer escape behavior; and any legal restriction
   on cultivating, importing, or moving the crop or its propagation material.

**Rows affected:** not yet moved. As of this date 39 rows were `RESEARCHED`
without this angle - `fruit` (32) and `algae` (7). Whether to mark them
`OUT-OF-DATE` per the checklist-versioning rule, or backfill this one line
in a separate pass, is still to be decided; update this entry when it is.
