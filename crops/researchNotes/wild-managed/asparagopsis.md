## Why it belongs in this category

- **Mixed case.** Feed supply is farmed in land tanks and ponds [3]. The
  Hawaiian food supply, limu kohu, is gathered wild by families at
  favoured "limu places" [4].
- **Managed, not just picked.** A Hawaii Sea Grant programme with Symbrosia
  and Kuaʻāina Ulu ʻAuamo grows stock and outplants it to restore
  populations [4]. That makes it a restoration-aquaculture case, between
  wild and farmed.

## Fields a wild-managed template would need, with limu kohu values

- **Harvest rule / technique:** Pinch off the branchlets without pulling up
  the base [4].
- **Habitat:** Reef edges with clean, well-flushed water [4].
- **Stock trend:** Became less common over the last 50 years [4].
- **Restocking:** Hatchery supply and outplanting (above) [4].
- **Who gathers:** Families, across generations [4]. Historically women's
  work [4].
- **Knowledge transfer:** Oral; has dwindled since Western influence [4].
- **Postharvest:** Keep in shade; salt and refrigerate, or freeze [4].
- **Tenure / licensing:** Not sourced in this pass.
