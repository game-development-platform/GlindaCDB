## Why it belongs in this category

- **Mostly wild.** Commercial supply is hand-harvested from wild beds [2];
  farming is only at trial stage [5].
- **Managed.** Licences set species, quota, method and area in British
  Columbia [2].

## Fields a wild-managed template would need, with bull kelp values

- **Harvest rule / technique:** Hand-cut only (BC). Prune at a set distance
  from holdfast or stipe [2]; cutting near the float kills the plant [2].
- **Timing rule:** Harvest after spore release, because the plant is
  annual [2].
- **Licence terms:** Species, quota, method, area; a harvest plan per
  species group and management area [2].
- **Jurisdiction contrast:** Strict in California and Washington; Oregon
  set no limits (older data) [2].
- **Stock trend:** Northern California lost over 90% of canopy from 2014
  [4].
- **Dependent fisheries:** Red abalone (closed 2018) and red urchin
  (collapsed) [4]. A template field for **dependent species** seems
  useful.
- **Restoration:** Nursery seeding and outplanting (Salish Sea) [4].
- **Scale:** About 20 permits and under 350 t a year across five
  jurisdictions (older data) [2].
