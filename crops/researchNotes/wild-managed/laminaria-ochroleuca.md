# Wild-managed resource notes: Golden kelp (*Laminaria ochroleuca*)

**Temporary file.** It holds research for the planned wild-managed-resource
template (see the GitHub issue linked from `README.md` in this folder). Once
that template exists, this content moves into a proper doc, and this file
is deleted.

**Source doc:** `crops/raw-data/algae/laminaria-ochroleuca.md` (researched
2026-11-16). The `[n]` numbers below refer to that doc's Sources list.

## Why it belongs in this category

- **Wild only.** No commercial farming found [5]; hand-harvested from
  natural beds [2].
- **Co-managed.** Galician plans are run jointly by government and fishing
  sector, and approved every three years [2].

## Fields a wild-managed template would need, with Galician values

- **Plan holder:** Fishers' guild (*confraría*) or processing firm [2].
- **Harvest method:** On foot, breath-hold diving, surface-supplied diving
  [2].
- **Daily cap:** Per boat, per on-foot harvester, or per diver (e.g. Vigo:
  1,000 kg/boat, 175 kg/person; Muros: 500 kg/diver) [2].
- **Season / days:** Calendar by plan; maximum days a year (e.g. 40) [2].
- **Permit count:** Fixed, sometimes closed to new entrants [2].
- **Control and sale:** Weighed at set control points; sold through named
  fish markets (*lonxas*) [2].
- **Plan term:** Triennial approval (2025–2027) [2].
- **Cutting rules:** Not stated in the Vigo plan [2].
- **Stock trend / range:** Expanding north with warming, replacing *L.
  hyperborea* in places [3].
- **Contrast with Norway:** Caps and permits instead of field rotation
  (`laminaria-hyperborea.md`).
