**Temporary file.** It holds research for the planned wild-managed-resource

**Source doc:** `crops/raw-data/algae/laminaria-pallida.md` (researched

same South African system covers *Ecklonia maxima* (`ecklonia.md`).

## Why it belongs in this category

- **Wild only.** Collected as beach-cast, under concessions [2][3].
- **Managed by concession.** South Africa's system is a tender for
  exclusive coastline areas [2].

## Fields a wild-managed template would need, with southern African values

- **Tenure:** 23 seaweed concession areas; quotas awarded for 10 years
  after a government tender [2].
- **Exclusions:** No harvest in Marine Protected Areas [2].
- **Harvest source:** Mostly beach-cast; supply follows storms [2][3].
- **Collection rule (Namibia operator):** Hand collection in the surf
  zone; blades and holdfasts left on the beach for wildlife [3].
- **Permits (Namibia):** Environmental clearance certificate and park
  permits [3]. Enforcement gaps: a firm accused of collecting without one
  [3].
- **Market shift:** Alginate wash-up exports have collapsed; abalone feed
  and agriculture now dominate kelp use [2][3].
