# Wild-managed resource notes: Huiro (*Lessonia* spp.), Chile

**Temporary file.** It holds research for the planned wild-managed-resource
template (see the GitHub issue linked from `README.md` in this folder). Once
that template exists, this content moves into a proper doc, and this file
is deleted.

**Source doc:** `crops/raw-data/algae/lessonia.md` (researched 2026-11-16).
The `[n]` numbers below refer to that doc's Sources list.

## Why it belongs in this category

- **Wild only.** Harvested from natural beds and shores [2]; no farming
  found.
- **Heavily managed.** Regional management plans, committees, quotas, bans
  and registered fisher lists [2].

## Fields a wild-managed template would need, with huiro values

- **Harvest modes:** Beach-cast collection (*varado*), prying off whole
  plants (*barreteo*), cutting (*segado*) [2].
- **Quota by mode:** Separate quotas for each mode, split by province and
  quarter [2].
- **Ban by mode:** Bans forbid active removal but allow beach-cast
  collection under quota [2].
- **Regime change:** Open access until 2013; management plans since (Law
  20.657) [2].
- **Who harvests:** Thousands of registered artisanal fishers (6,372 in
  the north) [2].
- **Tenure:** Open-access areas under plans, plus exclusive fishers'
  management areas (AMERB) [2].
- **Compliance problem:** Mode misreporting (483% vs 9% quota use) [2];
  illegal harvest and trafficking [2].
- **Scale:** Record 382,000 t of landings (2013) [2].
- **Recovery:** Best recruit growth after total harvest in autumn [3].
- **Ecological indicator:** A limpet association as a bed-health sign [3].
